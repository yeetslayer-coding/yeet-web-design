## Prompt

```text
Design an industrial equipment catalog for [BRAND] and [AUDIENCE], guiding users to [PRIMARY_ACTION], using [VERIFIED_CONTENT] and [APPROVED_ASSETS]. Adapt W017's mechanical silhouette, strong rectangular hierarchy and category-to-application sequence.

Use a restrained monochrome or approved industrial palette. Compose the hero around an accurate major machine or component, with a large category heading and carefully separated factual information. Give the object credible scale, support and contact shadow. Use compact technical labels and straightforward rectangular actions where appropriate.

Create a contrasting category band with relevant equipment images and fine separators, followed by an authentic application photograph showing what the equipment does. Then provide a comparable product grid with real model names, meaningful technical information and the actual inquiry, selection or purchase path.

Do not invent mechanical joints, cutting processes, performance ratings, certifications or prices. The source's AI-modified label makes technical accuracy especially important. Use approved product imagery or correct references for physical geometry. On phones, simplify the oversized heading, keep category labels readable and place equipment selection in a logical sequence. After plan approval, implement required catalog actions and verify technical content, focus states and narrow layouts.
```
