## Prompt

```text
Design a room- and collection-led page for [BRAND] and [AUDIENCE], using [VERIFIED_CONTENT] and [APPROVED_ASSETS], with [PRIMARY_ACTION]. Adapt W021's shift from a broad interior environment to isolated product forms.

Begin with a real contextual room photograph and concise typography placed in intentional negative space. Navigation and the primary action must remain legible against the image; do not reproduce the source's low-contrast text merely for softness. Transition into a light collection section with a small number of comparable product silhouettes on consistent circular or otherwise approved neutral fields.

Use accurate names and prices and provide a clear route to the full collection. Add a short factual benefits section only where needed, followed by a brand explanation connected to a complementary interior image. Keep warm materials and lighting coherent without applying a beige filter to every product.

On phones, select a useful room crop, use readable product scale, and stack supporting content while retaining the atmosphere-to-product sequence. Do not duplicate source labels or fabricate sustainability claims. No carousel or mobile behavior was observed. After plan approval, implement actual product destinations and any agreed collection controls, then verify contrast and responsive reading order.
```
